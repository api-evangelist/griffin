# Griffin

> Griffin is a UK-regulated bank (Financial Conduct Authority authorised, Prudential Regulation Authority regulated) that provides Banking as a Service APIs, allowing fintechs and businesses to embed bank accounts, payments, and financial products directly into their own platforms.

## Products

### Business banking
- [Business bank accounts](https://griffin.com/products/business-bank-accounts): Your own operational account

### Platform banking
- [Client money accounts](https://griffin.com/products/client-money): CASS and CMP compliant accounts for holding client funds
- [Safeguarding accounts](https://griffin.com/products/safeguarding): Ringfenced accounts for EMIs and PIs
- [Savings accounts](https://griffin.com/products/savings-accounts): Interest-bearing accounts for your customers
- [Embedded bank accounts](https://griffin.com/products/embedded-bank-accounts): Bank accounts to power any product

### Onboarding
- [Verify](https://griffin.com/products/verify): Automated customer onboarding (KYC/KYB)

### Payments
- [Payments](https://griffin.com/products/payments): API-enabled payment options including Faster Payments and BACS

## Solutions

- [Payroll](https://griffin.com/solutions/payroll): Embedded banking for payroll platforms
- [Proptech](https://griffin.com/solutions/proptech): Financial infrastructure for property technology companies
- [Lending](https://griffin.com/solutions/lending): Banking services for lending platforms
- [Wealthtech](https://griffin.com/solutions/wealthtech): Savings and investment account infrastructure
- [FX and remittance](https://griffin.com/solutions/fx-and-remittance): Accounts and payments for FX and remittance businesses
- [E-money and payments](https://griffin.com/solutions/e-money-and-payments): Banking infrastructure for e-money institutions and payment platforms
